# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Staff of the Provincial Assessor's Office. They use TDAMS at the office to scan tax declarations, file those scans, and look up a declaration again. The public does not use it.

## Product Purpose

TDAMS (Tax Declaration Archive Management System) is how the office keeps tax declaration scans with the physical archive they belong to, and finds a declaration later. Success is a staff member opening the right scan and knowing which cabinet, drawer, and book or folder holds it.

## Positioning

The archive on screen is the real filing floor. A scan stays tied to one cabinet, one drawer, and one book or folder, and to one tax declaration: its owner, its property, and the previous declaration. A plain document store does not file that way.

## Operating Context

Staff work in a signed-in desktop browser. They upload a scan, run OCR through a separate local service, and save only the declaration fields that are actually on the page. They organize storage as cabinets, drawers, and books or folders, then find a document again from the archive floor or from the declaration record. The office is the Provincial Assessor's Office of Ilocos Norte.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Signed-in staff only. Not a public site and not a phone app.
- Filing places are cabinets, drawers, and books or folders. A scan keeps that physical location. Each drawer holds only books or only folders, determined by its first item. Books and folders have separate management pages.
- A tax declaration record holds the owner, the property, and the previous declaration when those are on the scan.
- Never fill a declaration field the scan does not contain. Blank stays blank.
- OCR runs in a separate local service. The browser does not run OCR itself.
- The product name is TDAMS. The office name stays the Provincial Assessor's Office of Ilocos Norte.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is TDAMS, short for Tax Declaration Archive Management System. It belongs to the Provincial Assessor's Office of Ilocos Norte. The office mark is `public/logo.png`.

## Evidence on Hand

The running app is the evidence: dashboard, documents, archive floor, bookmarks, and cabinet, book, and folder management. There are no testimonials, case studies, or public claims to reuse. Do not invent property owners, declaration numbers, or office statistics.

## Product Principles

- Cabinets, drawers, and books or folders are the filing system, not a picture of one.
- Every scan belongs to one tax declaration, with its owner, property, and previous declaration when the page has them.
- Staff save what the document says. Unseen fields stay empty.
- The tool is for office staff, on a desktop browser, behind sign-in.
- OCR helps reading. It does not replace the staff check, and it stays outside the browser.
